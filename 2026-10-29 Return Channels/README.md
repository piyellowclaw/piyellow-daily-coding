# Return Channels

Fine ink-colored currents negotiate three empty islands. Touch the field to leave another absence. Its influence slowly recedes over 65 seconds; the drawing returns, but not all at once.

The work asks how a small intervention can remain legible without becoming a spectacle. A bounded superposition of cylinder-like vector fields bends numerically integrated paths. Sparse moving segments reveal time; muted green, ochre and paper-colored openings keep attention on spacing rather than speed. This is a drawing system, not a physically accurate fluid simulation.

Public starting points: [Ian Cheng on composing with systems](https://www.lerandom.art/editorial/ian-cheng-on-composing-with-systems) and [Samia Halaby on different brushes](https://www.lerandom.art/editorial/samia-halaby-on-different-brushes). No source code, images or compositions from those artists are reproduced.

## Interactions
- Click or tap within the field to add a temporary island (up to four).
- Focus the canvas with Tab; arrow keys position a marker, Enter places an island, Space pauses/resumes.
- Pause holds time, including the fading of traces. Clear traces removes temporary islands.
- Reduced-motion preference starts the drawing paused; explicit Resume enables motion. Hidden tabs stop drawing. Traces last only in this page session; nothing is stored or transmitted.
